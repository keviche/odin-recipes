Shoyu Chicken

Description

Shoyu Chicken is an iconic Hawaiian-Japanese fusion dish often served with rice. Many people read shoyu and immediately think of shoyu ramen, but never know that shoyu means soy sauce in japanese. Thus, we have essentially soy sauce marinated chicken that has flavors more complex than the name might suggest.

Ingredients

  • 5 lb skinless chicken thigh
  • 1 cup soy sauce
  • 1 cup brown sugar
  • 1 cup water
  • 1 chopped onion
  • 4 cloves minced garlic
  • 1 tbsp grated fresh ginger root
  • 1 tbsp ground black pepper
  • 1 tbsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp crushed red pepper flakes (optional)
  • 1 tsp ground paprika (optional)
  • 1 tsp ground cayenne pepper (optional)

Steps

  1. Whisk soy sauce, brown sugar, water, onion, garlic, ginger, black pepper, oregano, red pepper flakes, cayenne pepper, and paprika together in a large bowl
  2. Toss the chicken thighs into the bowl and coat evenly
  3. Cover the bowl with plastic wrap and marinate for minimum one hour (The longer you marinate the better!)
  4. Preheat a grill to medium heat, and lightly oil
  5. Grill the marinated thighs for about 15 mins per side with interior temp of at least 165 degrees fahrenheit. Center should not be pink and juices should run clear.
  6. Enjoy!
