German Chocolate Dump Cake

Description

If you like cake then this is the perfect recipe for you. Directions are easy to follow, and the finished product takes you to heaven. Moist and chocolately, with hints of pecan and coconut, all while having some crunchy bits that create a perfect balance of textures.

Ingredients

  • 1 and 2/3 cup chopped and toasted pecans
  • 1 cup sweetened flaked coconut
  • 1 package of the 2-layered devil's food cake mix
  • required ingredients for the cake mix
  • 8 ounces cream cheese
  • 1/2 cup melted butter
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 1 cup powdered sugar
  • 2/3 cup semisweet chocolate chips

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees fahrenheit
  2. Grease a 9x13 baking pan
  3. Sprinkle 1 cup coconut and pecans in the greased baking pan
  4. Prepare cake mix as specified by instructions, and pour in baking pan over the coconuts and pecans
  5. Beat cream cheese until smooth, then beat in melted butter, salt, and vanilla. Then beat in powdered sugar until smooth. Stir in chocolate chips and remaining 2/3 cup of pecans.
  6. Drop spoonfuls of the cream cheese miture over the cake batter and swirm them together
  7. Bake in oven until toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean. Approx. 32-38 min
  8. Cool on wire rack
  9. Enjoy!
